## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an intimate, high-editorial aesthetic that marries classical Bengal refinement with modern global luxury cosmetics retail (reminiscent of Sulwhasoo’s quiet poise and Sephora’s curated authority). 

### Target Audience & Emotional Tone
Designed for discerning beauty patrons seeking an elevated, bespoke shopping ritual. The interface evokes:
- **Sensual Restraint:** Generous breathing room, unhurried pacing, and tactile visual warmth.
- **Cultural Poise:** Subtle gold metallic notes and rich wine tones referencing celebratory adornment without falling into ornamental excess.
- **Dermatological & Curatorial Trust:** Clean, hyper-legible metadata, ingredient highlights, and architectural clarity.

### Style Movements
A hybrid of **Refined Warm Minimalism** and **Editorial Elegance**:
- Muted warm ivory foundations over sterile cool whites.
- Whispering hair-thin borders tinted in champagne gold.
- High-contrast serif headlines delivering dramatic narrative authority, grounded by clean contemporary humanist sans typography.

## Colors

The palette relies on tonal warmth, avoiding harsh pure blacks or stark blues.

- **Primary (`#4A0E17` — Imperial Wine):** Used for primary typography, editorial callouts, primary buttons, and key interactive focal points. Conveys luxury, authority, and deep pigment contrast against light canvases.
- **Secondary (`#C5A059` — Champagne Gold):** Reserved for delicate hairline dividers, verified badges, rating stars, hover borders, and active indicators. Adds metallic warmth without loud glitz.
- **Tertiary (`#F7EBE8` — Petal Blush):** Utilized for soft interactive states, pill tag backgrounds, promo banners, and gentle surface differentiation.
- **Neutral Canvas (`#FDFBF7` — Warm Ivory):** The base atmospheric layer. Paired with `#F5EFEB` (Muted Alabaster) for secondary stacked containers and sub-panels.
- **Text & Structure Hierarchy:**
  - High-Emphasis Headlines: `#360B12` (Midnight Burgundy)
  - Editorial Body: `#4A0E17` at 90% opacity or `#5C2830`
  - Subtle Labels & Meta: `#8C686F`
  - Hairline Rule Lines: `#C5A059` with 30%–45% alpha or solid `#E8DDD9`

## Typography

The typographic pairing balances poetic prestige with effortless transactional utility.

- **Editorial Serifs (`Playfair Display`):** Reserved for brand moments, category spotlights, hero slogans, and product names. Italicized cuts should be used selectively for sensory adjectives (e.g., *Hydrating*, *Velvet*, *Limited Edition*) to reinforce high-fashion magazine styling.
- **Modern Clean Sans (`Plus Jakarta Sans`):** Carries all utilitarian weight—ingredient lists, pricing, navigation bar items, ratings, and checkout flows. Its rounded geometric precision maintains high legibility on mobile devices while feeling soft and approachable.
- **Micro-Copy & Eyebrows:** Always employ `label-caps` in uppercase with wide tracking (`0.12em`) set in champagne gold or muted wine to create structured editorial pacing before major titles.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is grounded in a 12-column responsive fluid grid designed to frame beauty items like museum artifacts.

### Breakpoints & Geometry
- **Desktop (1200px+):** 12 columns, `margin: 3.5rem`, `gutter: 1.5rem`. Max container width capped at 1440px to retain intimate, balanced framing.
- **Tablet (768px – 1199px):** 8 columns, `margin: 2rem`, `gutter: 1.25rem`.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4 columns, `margin: 1.25rem`, `gutter: 1rem`. Product cards default to a structured 2-column masonry or horizontal snap-carousel with partial peek.

### Rhythmic Rules
- **Vertical Air:** Section transitions use alternating generous vertical gaps (`space-xl` * 2) to prevent visual density.
- **Asymmetric Offsets:** Selected hero showcases utilize asymmetric multi-column overlaps where editorial imagery flows slightly outside standard content bounds behind typography layers.

## Elevation & Depth

This system intentionally rejects heavy, dark drop shadows in favor of ambient luminescence and tonal layering.

### Surface Tiers
- **Canvas Base:** Solid `#FDFBF7`.
- **Tier 1 (Surface Elevated):** `#FFFFFF` paired with an ultra-soft ambient bloom: `box-shadow: 0 8px 30px rgba(74, 14, 23, 0.04)`.
- **Tier 2 (Floating Overlays & Drawers):** `#FFFFFF` edged with a hairline border (`1px solid rgba(197, 160, 89, 0.25)`) and a diffused shadow: `box-shadow: 0 16px 40px rgba(54, 11, 18, 0.08)`.

### Hairline Rules & Micro-Framing
Depth is predominantly defined by architectural lines:
- Delicate 1px borders tinted in soft blush (`#EED7D1`) or champagne gold (`#C5A059` at 35% opacity) delineate cards and headers.
- Interactive states lift elements with a crisp 1px pure gold transition rather than elevated z-axis jumps.

## Shapes

The foundational shape philosophy is **Soft Architectural (`roundedness: 1`)**. 

- **Cards & Visual Containers:** Sculpted with a disciplined 0.25rem (`4px`) to 0.5rem (`8px`) corner radius, maintaining a refined box-edge geometry reminiscent of luxury cosmetic packaging.
- **Pill Exceptions:** Badges, tags, filter chips, and circular icon triggers (wishlist buttons) are purposefully exempted from structural rectilinear rules and styled as full rounded capsules (`9999px`) to create tactile, soft contact points.
- **Image Aspect Ratios:** Strict vertical proportions (`3:4` or `4:5`) for product captures and editorial spreads, echoing beauty portraiture.

## Components

### 1. Announcement Bar
- **Surface:** Deep Wine (`#4A0E17`) with Champagne Gold (`#D4AF37`) micro-text.
- **Typography:** `label-caps` set at 11px uppercase with `0.15em` letter-spacing.
- **Details:** Centered bespoke messaging (e.g., "Complimentary Luxury Samples with Every Order in Haldia") accompanied by slim separator diamonds (`◈`).

### 2. Product Cards
- **Container:** Warm ivory or white backdrop, bordered by a subtle 1px border (`#F0E5E1`).
- **Media Frame:** Vertical `4:5` ratio with soft blush inner matting (`#FAF3F0`). Smooth hover zoom (`scale-105`) over 500ms ease.
- **Wishlist Trigger:** Circular floating pill button in top-right corner; backdrop blur (`rgba(253, 251, 247, 0.8)`), champagne gold heart stroke, filled wine on active state.
- **Badges:** Pill-shaped capsules positioned top-left (`label-caps`), such as "EXCLUSIVE" (soft blush surface with wine text) or "CURATOR'S CHOICE" (gold foil outline).
- **Metadata:**
  - Brand name: `label-caps` in `#8C686F`.
  - Title: `title-editorial` in `#360B12`.
  - Rating: 5-star cluster rendered in `#C5A059` alongside review counts in `body-sm`.
  - Price: Bold sans figure in `#4A0E17`, with strikethrough original price in muted tone.

### 3. Buttons
- **Primary:** Deep Burgundy fill (`#4A0E17`), crisp ivory text (`#FDFBF7`), subtle 0.25rem radius. Hover shifts to `#360B12` with a 1px inner glow of gold.
- **Secondary / Editorial:** Transparent fill, 1px solid Champagne Gold (`#C5A059`), dark wine text. Hover transitions to blush wash (`#F7EBE8`).
- **Text Action:** `label-caps` with a permanent 1px gold underline offset by 4px, animating outward on hover.

### 4. Chips & Filters
- **Form:** Full pill shape (`border-radius: 9999px`).
- **Default:** `#F5EFEB` background, no border, `#4A0E17` text.
- **Selected:** Wine fill (`#4A0E17`), ivory text, accompanied by an optional small gold dot indicator.

### 5. Input Fields
- **Base Style:** Floating label pattern with a warm ivory background (`#FFFFFF` on ivory canvas).
- **Border:** Underline-only or full 1px hairline border in `#E8DDD9`.
- **Focus:** Crisp transition to Champagne Gold (`#C5A059`) border with zero heavy glow.

### 6. Checkboxes & Radio Buttons
- **Checkboxes:** 18px square with 2px soft radius, gold border (`#C5A059`). Checked state fills with `#4A0E17` displaying an ivory checkmark.
- **Radios:** 18px concentric circle; active state shows a centered Champagne Gold dot encased in deep wine.
